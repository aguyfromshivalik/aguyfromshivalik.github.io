Ciao!

I am working at University of Padova (UNiPD) with Dr. Sergio Rampino. My research is in the field of reaction dynamics and exploration of ML applications in Chemistry. I obtained my PhD in Theoretical Chemistry under the guidance of Prof. Susanta Mahapatra from University of Hyderabad, Telangana, India. I come from Uttarakhand, India.
